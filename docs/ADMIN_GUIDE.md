# Obliskia Admin Quick Guide

Welcome to the team! Here's what you need to know.

## Moderating players
- **Small mistake:** Explain the rule and help fix it.
- **Spam:** Ask them to stop; warn them if it continues.
- **Griefing, stealing, cheating, or duplication:** Save evidence and contact the owner if it's serious or repeated.
- **Harassment or threats:** Protect the affected player and tell the owner quickly.
- **Lost items or bugs:** Get the details before promising a replacement.

Don't punish people based on rumors. For serious incidents, note what happened, when, who was involved, and what you did. Keep those notes private.

## Keep the world safe
- Only give admin powers to people who need them.
- Don't abuse powers to help yourself or friends.
- Don't share passwords, API keys, or private server info.
- Don't try risky commands or add-on changes on the live world without checking with the owner.
- Be careful with anything that clears inventories, changes permissions, or affects the world.

## Magic and commands
These values came up during development, so **check what's actually working in-game** before promising players anything:

- **MP:** 48 max; 1 MP regenerates every 2 seconds.
- **Fireball:** 8 MP.
- **Flamethrower:** Sneak to use; 1 MP every 10 ticks.
- **Ring of Fire:** 25 MP; radius 5; 10 seconds.
- **Black Hole:** “100%” cost was discussed, but the exact rule needs checking.
- **Dash:** 20-block slow field for 1 second was discussed.
- **Tome required:** A tome was part of the planned spell system.

`/sethome`, `/home`, and `/tpa` have been mentioned, but their availability and syntax aren't confirmed. Test first.

If something breaks, write down what happened, gather screenshots/details, and test in a separate world. Don't edit scoreboards or functions unless you know what they do.

## AI wizard
The AI wizard is a development project; don't assume it's running in the live world. Test AI changes separately, keep its permissions limited, and get human approval before destructive actions.

## Arcane Ledger / equipment menu
**Not ready for the live world.** It's still a prototype. Don't install it or hand out placeholder equipment until the owner says it's ready.

## Before you go
- Know what you're allowed to do.
- Know how to contact the owner.
- Keep private info private.
- If you're unsure, pause and ask instead of guessing.
